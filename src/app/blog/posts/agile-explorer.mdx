# Embracing the Agile Mindset

*"The best way to get started is to quit talking and begin doing."* - Walt Disney

## What This Achievement Means 📘

I earned the **Agile Explorer** credential from IBM as part of **BCU module CMP5322 - Enterprise Practice Project**, which links directly to my final year work. It covers Agile values, principles, and practices: how teams iterate, adapt, and deliver in steps rather than in one big bang at the end.

## The Agile Advantage ⚡

<Text>

## Key Agile Principles 🧩

### **Individuals and interactions** over processes and tools 🎯
- Communication and collaboration first
- Strong team relationships
- People before paperwork

### **Working software** over comprehensive documentation ⚡
- Deliver value early
- Focus on what users can actually use
- Improve based on feedback

### **Customer collaboration** over contract negotiation 🤝
- Keep stakeholders involved
- Adapt when requirements change
- Treat it as a partnership

### **Responding to change** over following a plan 🔄
- Plans are useful; rigidity is not
- Learn from each iteration
- Treat uncertainty as normal

## Where I Use It 🛠️

- **Project management**: Breaking work into sprints
- **Team work**: Stand-ups and retrospectives in group projects
- **Problem solving**: Trying small fixes before big rewrites
- **Learning**: Reviewing what worked after each milestone

## The Journey Continues 🚀

The credential is a starting point. Agile is mostly a habit: ship something, learn, adjust. I am applying that on my final year project and anywhere else the team will tolerate stand-ups.
