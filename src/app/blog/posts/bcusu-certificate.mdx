## A Recognition Worth Celebrating 🏅

I've received a **Certificate of Appreciation** from the [Birmingham City University Students' Union (BCUSU)](http://Bcusu.com) for serving as a course representative during the 2023-2024 academic year.

## What the Certificate Says 📝

The official recognition reads:

> *"Thank you for being a course rep this year. Your contributions are an important part of your Students' Union and University feedback system. By representing [your course](https://www.dagomirwojcik.com/education/bcu/), you are a key part of the Representation System. We depend on people like you, to represent student voices, so we are able to act on behalf of all students in their best interests."*

## Why This Matters 💡

Being a course representative at Birmingham City University was more than a title on paper. In practice it meant:

- **Amplifying student voices** – making sure concerns and suggestions reached staff
- **Bridging the gap** – linking students and university administration
- **Driving positive change** – using BCUSU's representation system to improve things for the cohort

## The Impact of Student Representation 🤝

The certificate is recognition that student voice actually shapes how courses run. BCUSU relies on reps who will listen, pass feedback on clearly, and advocate for sensible improvements.

## Looking Forward 🔮

It was a good year of representing my coursemates, and I'm grateful to BCUSU for the acknowledgement. Student representation only works when people speak up; I'm glad I could play that role.

*Thank you to BCUSU, and to everyone on the course who trusted me to represent them.*
