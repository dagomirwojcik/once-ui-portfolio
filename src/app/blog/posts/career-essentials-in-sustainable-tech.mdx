## What is Career Essentials in Sustainable Technology? 🌱

The Career Essentials in Sustainable Technology certification by Microsoft and LinkedIn covers how technology can support sustainable change. It looks at sustainability concepts that matter to businesses, how sustainability can drive innovation, and the tech solutions companies use to meet their goals.

## My Sustainable Technology Journey 🚀

I did this course on my own initiative, outside my Business Information Technology degree requirements. I wanted a clearer picture of how tech fits into environmental and social responsibility, and it sits well alongside the business side of my studies.

The material pushed me to think about how organisations use technology for things like reducing carbon footprint and social impact, not just which tools to pick. Understanding the principles behind those decisions was more useful than memorising product names.

## Key Skills Developed 💡

Through the Career Essentials programme, I picked up:

- **Sustainability concepts**: ESG principles and how they show up in business
- **Technology for good**: How tech can address sustainability challenges
- **Innovation drivers**: Why sustainability can be a business advantage, not just a cost
- **Strategic implementation**: Planning sustainability-focused tech initiatives
- **Impact measurement**: Frameworks and metrics for tracking progress
- **Microsoft sustainability tools**: An overview of Microsoft's platforms in this space

## How I've Used It 🔧

This has come in handy on academic work and when talking to people outside my immediate team:

- Considering environmental and social impact when designing solutions
- Understanding the business case for sustainability in tech projects
- Communicating with sustainability or ESG stakeholders
- Applying sustainable practices in development and rollout

## What's Next? 🔮

I'm not aiming for a dedicated sustainability role, but the topics keep appearing in business IT work: green tech, CSR initiatives, digital transformation with ESG in mind, and working with sustainability teams. Having this foundation makes those conversations easier.
