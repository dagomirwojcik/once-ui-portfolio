## What is Career Essentials in System Administration? 🖥️

The Career Essentials in System Administration certification by Microsoft and LinkedIn gives an overview of the sysadmin role and the skills needed to manage modern IT infrastructure: on-premises, cloud, physical, and virtual environments.

It covers storage security, infrastructure management, and the basics you'd need to start or progress in system administration.

## My System Administration Journey 🚀

I completed this course for my own development. It wasn't required for my Business Information Technology degree, but it filled a gap: I needed a better grasp of how organisations actually run their infrastructure day to day.

The course walked through everything from traditional on-prem setups to cloud, and helped me see why reliability, security, and efficiency matter in business contexts, not just in theory.

## Key Skills Developed 💡

Through the Career Essentials programme, I covered:

- **System administration fundamentals**: Role, responsibilities, and typical workflows
- **Infrastructure management**: On-premises, cloud, physical, and virtual setups
- **Storage security**: Data protection and storage management
- **IT operations**: Monitoring, maintenance, and troubleshooting
- **Career development**: Paths into and through sysadmin roles
- **Microsoft technologies**: Tools and platforms in the Microsoft ecosystem

## How I've Used It 🔧

The knowledge has been useful on academic projects and when working with IT teams:

- Designing infrastructure for business-oriented projects
- Understanding operational requirements for enterprise systems
- Communicating with ops and sysadmin colleagues
- Applying security basics in system design

## What's Next? 🔮

I'm not training to be a full-time sysadmin, but infrastructure topics keep coming up: business tech solutions, enterprise integration, digital transformation, and collaboration with IT operations. This certification gives me enough context to contribute properly in those situations.
