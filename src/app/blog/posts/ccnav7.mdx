## What is CCNAv7? 🌐

The Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) v7 Introduction to Networks is the entry point to Cisco's certification track. It covers network fundamentals: IP addressing, routing, switching, and security. It's aimed at network administrators, engineers, and help desk roles.

## My Networking Journey 🚀

I earned CCNAv7 as part of **CMP4269 - Networking Fundamentals** at Birmingham City University. As a Business Information Technology student, it gave me proper networking foundations to go with the business side of my degree.

The course made me follow how data actually moves through networks, from a home setup to enterprise environments. Memorising protocols only gets you so far; understanding why things are designed the way they are is what stuck.

## Key Skills Developed 💡

- **Network fundamentals**: OSI and TCP/IP, topologies, protocols
- **IP addressing**: IPv4 and IPv6, subnetting, VLSM
- **Routing & switching**: Routing protocols, VLANs, segmentation
- **Security concepts**: Network security principles and good practice
- **Troubleshooting**: A structured approach to finding and fixing issues
- **Cisco technologies**: Hands-on work with Cisco IOS and device configuration

## How I've Used It 🔧

- Designing more secure network layouts for projects
- Troubleshooting connectivity in dev environments
- Talking to network and IT teams without getting lost
- Applying security basics in application work

## What's Next? 🔮

I'm not chasing further Cisco certs right now, but networking still matters for business IT work, enterprise integration, and any project where infrastructure and connectivity come up. CCNAv7 is a solid base for that.
