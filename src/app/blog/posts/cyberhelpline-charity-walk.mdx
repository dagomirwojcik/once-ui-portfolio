## Walking for a Cause 🚶🏻‍♂️

On a Sunday in October I joined a charity walk for [The Cyber Helpline](https://www.thecyberhelpline.org/), which supports people affected by cybercrime. A long walk around Birmingham felt like a straightforward way to raise money and awareness for something that matters in the work I care about.

## The Mission 🎯

The Cyber Helpline helps victims of online crime: fraud, harassment, identity theft, and similar cases. The walk was about funding that support and reminding people the service exists.

## The Journey 🚶

We covered **10+ miles** around Birmingham. It was tiring but straightforward: walk, talk, donate if you can.

### Highlights ✨

- **Distance**: 10+ miles
- **Purpose**: Fundraising for cybercrime victims
- **Who was there**: Mix of tech people and supporters who wanted to help

## Making a Difference 💛

Money raised goes towards:

- Immediate help for people reporting cybercrime
- Guidance through recovery
- Awareness of prevention
- Practical support after an attack

## The Experience 🤝

Walking with people who care about the same issue made the distance easier. Cybercrime affects more people than we talk about openly; charities like this fill a gap when someone does not know where to turn.

## Looking Forward 🔮

The walk was one day, but the helpline's work continues. I am glad I could contribute in a small way and would do it again.
