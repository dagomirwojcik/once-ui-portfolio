I completed the "Digital Skills: Artificial Intelligence" course on FutureLearn, delivered with Accenture. It is a practical, business-focused introduction to AI: foundations, real applications, and the ethical side of using these tools responsibly.

### What I Learned 🧠
- **AI fundamentals**: Clear definitions of AI vs. machine learning vs. deep learning, and where they fit in real systems.
- **Core techniques**: Supervised and unsupervised learning, classification vs. regression, and model evaluation at a high level.
- **Data and lifecycle**: How data quality, features, and iteration matter more than picking the fanciest algorithm.
- **Responsible AI**: Bias, fairness, transparency, and privacy, plus frameworks to reduce risk.
- **Business impact**: Use cases in customer service, operations, analytics, and product work.
- **Careers in AI**: Roles across data, engineering, product, and ethics, and which skills transfer between them.

### Hands-On Elements 🛠️
The course uses short case studies and guided activities on framing AI problems, checking data readiness, and balancing value with risk. I found the focus on problem framing and aligning with stakeholders especially useful for actual projects.

### Why It Matters to My Work 💼
It helped me spot sensible, ethical AI opportunities and explain trade-offs to non-technical people. It also reinforced a simple approach: start small, measure impact, scale only when it makes sense.

### Key Takeaways ✅
- **Start with the problem, not the model.**
- **Good data beats clever algorithms.**
- **Ethics is a requirement, not an afterthought.**
- **Explainability builds trust.**

Proud to have the certificate and to keep these principles in mind on future work.

### Course at a Glance 📘
- **Duration**: 3 weeks, part-time, fully online
- **Created by**: Accenture, on FutureLearn
- **Credential**: Free digital certificate (CPD accredited) on completion

### Syllabus Overview 🧭
- **Week 1: Introduction to AI** – Origins and evolution of AI and generative AI, current applications, and where AI shows up in daily life and work.
- **Week 2: AI in industry** – How AI affects sectors and roles, with examples of opportunities and risks.
- **Week 3: Working with AI** – Human–AI collaboration, future trends, and practical ways to adapt your skills.

### Outcomes I Gained 🏆
- Foundational literacy across AI, ML, and generative AI
- Awareness of where AI creates value (and where it does not)
- A responsible AI mindset around bias, privacy, and transparency
- A clearer plan for upskilling in a measured, ethical way
