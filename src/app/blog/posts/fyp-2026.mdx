## Wrapping Up Undergraduate Study 🎓

Innovation Fest 2026 fell in my final week as an undergraduate at Birmingham City University. It felt like a fitting way to round off that chapter.

## Innovation Fest 2026 🚀

Innovation Fest is BCU's annual showcase for student work in computing, engineering, and related fields. Like every year, it was held at **STEAMhouse**, with projects on display and plenty of opportunity to talk through what people had built.

For me, the fest was also where I completed my **final year project viva**, presenting and defending the work I had spent the last year on.

## The Project: Healthcare Micro-Architecture 🏥

I presented **Towards a Scalable and Modular System Architecture for Healthcare SMEs: A Microservice Approach**. It follows the same healthcare micro-architecture thread I pitched live on stage at Innovation Fest 2025, now taken through to dissertation depth.

After so much time researching, designing, and developing it, it was rewarding to finally bring everything together. The poster behind me in the photo was a good way to reflect on the journey behind the architecture, not just the final write-up.

## Dr Arshad Mahmood Memorial Prize 🏆

I am also incredibly grateful and honoured to have been awarded the **Dr Arshad Mahmood Memorial Prize**, celebrating me as a very supportive student.

That recognition meant a lot. Supporting others through university, whether on modules, projects, or day-to-day help, is something I have tried to keep doing alongside my own work.

## More Than My Own Stand 💡

Beyond my own viva, it was interesting to see the creativity behind the first-year projects and to listen to a few of the second-year pitches. I also got to see what some of the students I have worked with had been building towards.

Days like this are a reminder of how much goes on across the university at once.
