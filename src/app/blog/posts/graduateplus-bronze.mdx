## Graduate+ Bronze 🏅

The Graduate+ Bronze Award at Birmingham City University is earned by completing 12 tasks focused on employability and personal development. I did mine as part of **CMP4285**.

## What Is Graduate+ Bronze? 💼

It recognises students who go beyond core modules to build skills employers care about: CVs, LinkedIn, teamwork, careers sessions, and meetings with tutors or coaches.

## The 12 Tasks I Completed 📋

### Core Graduate+ Activities 🧩

**1. Complete a Library Induction** 📚  
Learning how to use the library properly (digital and physical resources).

**2. Create a LinkedIn Profile - Biomedical Science/Engineering** 💼  
First draft of a professional LinkedIn profile for my field.

**3. Upload your CV onto CV360 [CMP4285]** 📄  
Uploaded and refined my CV on the CV360 platform.

**4. Attend Welcome Week and/or Grad+ Week/Refresh for Success - Sport Courses** 🏃‍♂️  
Took part in Welcome Week and Graduate+ Week activities.

**5. PI - Potentially Profile Sports** ⚽  
Engaged with the Potentially Profile Sports initiative.

**6. Attend a Meeting with your Personal Tutor (1)** 👨‍🏫  
Met my personal tutor to review progress and goals.

### CMP4285 Bespoke Activities 🧠

**7. CMP4285 Innovation Project - Team Building** 🤝  
Team-based innovation project work.

**8. Credly - How to Create a Digital Portfolio (Task 1)** 💻  
Training on building a digital portfolio with Credly.

**9. LinkedIn Profile Amended** ✏️  
Updated LinkedIn based on feedback.

**10. Design Thinking (Acclaim Badge filed with Credly) - Task 2** 🎨  
Design Thinking module with badge on Credly.

**11. LinkedIn or Locked Out? - Careers Session** 🔓  
Careers session on LinkedIn and professional online presence.

**12. Attend a Meeting with your Personal Tutor/Thrive Tutor/Careers Coach** 🎯  
Further meeting with tutor or careers support.

## What I Got Out of It 🌱

- Better professional networking habits
- A clearer digital portfolio and LinkedIn presence
- More confidence working in teams on innovation tasks
- Practical careers advice, not only theory
- Design thinking tools I have reused on other modules

## Looking Forward 🔮

Bronze was the first step in BCU's Graduate+ pathway. The tasks felt like admin at times, but they forced me to do things I would otherwise have put off (CV, LinkedIn, careers conversations).
