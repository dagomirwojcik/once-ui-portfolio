## Graduate+ Silver 🏅

Graduate+ Silver builds on Bronze with four more involved categories. I completed mine in late 2024 and early 2025.

## What Is Graduate+ Silver? 💼

It is BCU's next tier of employability recognition: deeper activities, often with real tools or work experience, rather than one-off tick-box tasks.

## The Four Silver Categories 🧩

**1. Silver - Global Outlook** 🌍  
*Completed: 28 November 2024*  
Worked with the QLIKsense package with a focus on data literacy: analysis and visualisation skills that apply across different business contexts.

**2. Silver - Creative Problem Solving** 🧠  
*Completed: 4 December 2024*  
Further work with QLIKsense, using data tools to approach problems from different angles.

**3. Silver - Enterprising** 💡  
*Completed: 4 December 2024*  
Completed a FutureLearn course on my own initiative, outside mandatory module hours.

**4. Silver - Professional and Work Ready** 💼  
*Completed: 4 December 2024*  
Part-time work alongside my degree, applying what I was learning in a real workplace.

## What I Got Out of It 🌱

- Stronger data literacy with professional tooling
- More practice breaking problems down before jumping to solutions
- Proof that I will seek learning outside formal requirements
- Workplace experience that made module theory feel more concrete
- A bit more confidence talking about skills in interviews

## Looking Forward 🔮

Silver sits between Bronze and Gold on the Graduate+ track. The mix of data skills, self-directed learning, and paid work was more demanding than Bronze, and more useful day to day.
