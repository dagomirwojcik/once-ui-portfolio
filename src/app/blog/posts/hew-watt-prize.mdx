## A Prestigious Recognition 🏆

I was awarded the **Hew Watt Prize**, which recognises students who have made a strong contribution to the cultural and social life of the college and shown exceptional kindness to others.

## What Is the Hew Watt Prize? 🏆

The prize goes to students who engage with college life, support their peers, and help build an inclusive environment. It is not purely about grades; it is about the positive impact you have on people around you.

### Criteria for Recognition 📌
- Outstanding contribution to college cultural and social life
- Demonstrated kindness and support for fellow students
- Community leadership and positive influence
- Commitment to an inclusive, supportive environment

## What It Meant to Me 💬

Receiving the Hew Watt Prize was personal recognition, but it also reflected something I care about: **community and supporting others** at college. Education is not only individual results; it is also how you help others grow and feel included.

### Values the Award Recognises 🌟
- Community spirit and working with others
- Empathy and kindness in day-to-day interactions
- Taking part in cultural and social life at college
- Leading through helping people, not just titles

## Key Takeaways ✅

- Contributing to college life matters as much as your own results
- Small acts of support add up over time
- Getting involved in college culture benefits everyone
- You can lead by being reliable and kind, not only by being loud

## Looking Forward 🔮

The award reminded me to keep prioritising community and peer support as I move through university and into work. Success, for me, includes lifting others up and being someone people can rely on.
