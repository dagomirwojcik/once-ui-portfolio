## Managing Up: What It Actually Means 🧭

Some people seem to progress faster than others with similar technical skills. A lot of that comes down to **managing up**: working well with your manager so you both succeed.

It is not about flattery. It is about understanding what your boss needs, communicating clearly, and making their job easier without losing sight of your own goals.

## What Is Managing Up? 💼

In practice it means building a productive relationship with your line manager: aligning on priorities, flagging problems early, and showing your work in a way they can use when advocating for you.

## Why It Matters 💡

Technical ability gets you in the door. After that, you often need to:

- **Navigate how the organisation actually works**
- **Build trust with leadership**
- **Be visible for the right reasons**
- **Turn good work into opportunities**

## What I Took From the Course 📚

### 1. Learn Your Manager's Style 🧭
People lead differently. Some want brevity and numbers; others want context and discussion. Some are process-heavy; others care mainly about outcomes. Matching how you communicate to their style saves friction.

### 2. Communicate on Purpose 📣
- Raise issues with a suggested direction, not only complaints
- Use the format they prefer (email, chat, short meetings)
- Update them enough that there are no surprises, without overloading them

### 3. Build Trust Through Delivery 🤝
- Do what you say you will do
- Flag blockers early
- Be honest when you are stuck
- Keep quality consistent

### 4. Office Politics Without the Drama ⚖️
"Politics" here mostly means understanding who influences what. Useful habits:
- Know people outside your immediate team
- Notice informal decision-makers
- Stay out of unnecessary conflicts
- Tie your work to shared goals, not personal scores

## Personal Impact 🌱

The course changed how I prepare for meetings with managers and tutors. The main lesson: **if your manager (or supervisor) looks good because you are reliable and clear, that usually helps you too.**

## Key Takeaways ✅

✅ Relationships matter as much as technical skills  
✅ Learn how your manager likes to work  
✅ You can navigate politics without being underhand  
✅ Consistent delivery builds trust  
✅ Think about the wider organisation, not only your task list
