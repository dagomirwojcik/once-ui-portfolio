## The Challenge: Academic Struggles in Year 11 🧭

March 2020 turned education upside down. As a Year 11 student I was dealing with the pandemic and with grades that had been slipping for a while.

### The Reality Check 🧭

At the start of Year 11 I was underachieving badly: failing some subjects and low on motivation. Teachers believed I could do better; I did not feel the same, especially with GCSEs coming up fast.

In November I tried to stick to a simple rule: **do not let mistakes weigh you down. Notice them, learn from them, and move on.** That mindset underpinned everything that came next.

## The Transformation: From Struggling to Succeeding 🌱

### Strategic Focus and Revision 📚

By January 2020 I was on a proper schedule:
- **Three 45-minute study sessions a day**
- **After-school revision** most days
- **Time for other things** too, like working out

I realised I could spread myself thin across every subject or focus hard on the ones that mattered most for what I wanted next. I chose focus.

### Results 📈

- **Most subjects roughly doubled** in mock grades
- **Business Studies**: from grade 2 to grade 8
- **Maths and English**: hit the grade 6 (B) targets I needed
- **Five more GCSEs** on top of that

## Lockdown: A Strange but Useful Period 🧠

### Initial Doubts 🤔

When lockdown was announced, a classmate said something like *"If I'd known this five years ago I wouldn't have bothered revising."* For a moment that made me wonder if the effort had been pointless.

It had not. The discipline and habits from the turnaround were exactly what I used during lockdown.

### Finding an Interest in Technology 💻

With more time at home I leaned into tech properly:

- **Experimenting** with computing topics I had not touched before
- **Buying a Raspberry Pi** and learning to code on it
- **Building productivity systems**: calendars, to-do lists, timetables
- **Routines** to avoid drifting and procrastinating

### August 2020 📅

That month I deliberately cut back on social media, finished VLE work before college started, and prioritised sleep and structure. The organisation skills from that summer still help me now.

## Starting College 🎓

September 2020 felt like a fresh start:
- Teachers could see the improvement
- I became a **Communications Champion** at Palmer's College
- I made new friends and reconnected with old ones
- I learned that **grades are not enough on their own**: organisation, persistence, and motivation matter just as much

## What I Took From It 🌱

- A difficult period can still push you in a good direction if you respond deliberately
- I found a lasting interest in technology
- Planning and routines are not optional extras for me
- Self-directed learning works when you give it time
- Focus beats trying to fix everything at once

## Closing Thought 💭

Lockdown was hard for a lot of people, and I do not pretend it was easy. For me, though, it came after a year of turning school around, and it became time to build skills and confidence I still use today.
