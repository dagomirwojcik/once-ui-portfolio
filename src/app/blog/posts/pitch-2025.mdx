## What is Innovation Fest? 🚀

Innovation Fest is Birmingham City University's annual showcase for student projects in computing, engineering, and the built environment. This year it ran **19–23 May 2025**, with work on display across campus and chances to talk to industry people.

It is a good place to show what you have built, get feedback, and meet others working on similar problems.

## From 300 Students to the Stage 🎯

As part of my course I recorded a pitch for my final year project on healthcare micro-architecture. I expected that to be the end of it.

Then I was **selected from over 300 students** to present **live on stage**. So instead of a recorded submission, I stood in front of an audience and walked through the project in person.

## The Project: Healthcare Micro-Architecture 🏥

The project looks at a healthcare micro-architecture approach to real problems in medical technology. It is academic work, but the aim is something that could matter outside the classroom.

The live pitch was nerve-wracking and useful. Explaining technical ideas to a mixed audience, and taking questions from people in industry, forced me to tighten how I present the work.

## What's Next? 🔮

I am continuing the project and hope to bring fuller dissertation results to next year's fest. The experience confirmed that I want to keep working in healthcare technology, and that presenting your work clearly is a skill worth practising.
