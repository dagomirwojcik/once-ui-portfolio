## A New Chapter Begins 🚀

After months of planning, tweaking the design, and actually building the thing, my portfolio is live at **dagomirwojcik.com**.

I'm a Technology Specialist and Business Information Technology student at Birmingham City University, and this site is where I keep my projects, certifications, and blog posts in one place. It felt like the right time to pull it all together properly.

## The Technical Foundation & Design Philosophy ⚡🎨

The stack is **Next.js 15 & React 19** for server-side rendering, the App Router, and built-in optimisations. The **Once UI System** handles the design system, component library, and theming (including dark mode).

Visually, I went for a dark theme with slate neutrals, blue brand colours, and cyan accents, using the **Geist font family** throughout. There are reveal animations, letter effects, and transitions where they felt useful rather than everywhere.

The site includes an About page with my background and work experience, a project gallery with filtering, certification highlights (including CCNAv7), and MDX-powered blog posts. There's a contact form, social links, and navigation that works on mobile as well as desktop.

## Building with Cursor AI 🤖

I used **Cursor AI** heavily during development. It sped things up with context-aware suggestions, TypeScript help, and pointers on component structure. It also pushed me to learn React patterns, CSS layout techniques, and Next.js features like the App Router and Server Components that I might have put off otherwise.

A portfolio like this would have taken me much longer without that assistance. I'm happy with how it turned out.

## What It's For 🌟

The site is mainly for employers, collaborators, and anyone curious about what I've been working on at BCU. It ties my coursework to real projects and gives me somewhere to write up what I learn.

I'm planning to add more project write-ups, technical posts, and maybe some case studies as I go. If you're on a similar path, I hope some of it is useful.

## Conclusion ✨

Getting this live feels like a proper milestone. **Next.js**, the **Once UI System**, and **Cursor AI** made it achievable without cutting corners on quality.
