## What I Learned 💡

### Root Cause Analysis 🔎
You cannot fix a problem properly until you know what is causing it, and there is often more than one cause. The course covered:

- **Systems thinking**: When the visible problem is a symptom of something bigger
- **The 5 Whys**: Asking "why" repeatedly to get past surface answers
- **Fishbone diagrams**: Mapping causes and effects
- **Process mapping**: Spotting bottlenecks in how work flows

### Solution Generation 🛠️
Ways to come up with options before you commit:

- **Mind mapping**: Brainstorming visually
- **Decision trees**: Laying out choices and likely outcomes
- **Lateral thinking**: Stepping away from the obvious first idea
- **SCAMPER**: Structured ways to adapt existing solutions

### Decision Making ⚖️
How to pick between options:

- **Pros and cons**: Simple but effective
- **Decision matrix**: Scoring options against criteria
- **Using intuition carefully**: Gut feel plus evidence
- **Risk assessment**: What could go wrong with each choice

## Key Takeaways ✅

1. Many problems are symptoms of deeper issues
2. There is usually more than one workable solution
3. Structured techniques still leave room for creativity
4. Good decisions mix analysis with judgement
5. Writing things down helps you learn for next time
