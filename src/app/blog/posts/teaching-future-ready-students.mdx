## What is Teaching Future-Ready Students? 🎓

"Teaching Future-Ready Students" by Kevin Brookhouser is a LinkedIn Learning course on preparing learners for a fast-changing world. It focuses on three literacies: business, video production, and coding, plus strategies for project-based learning and real-world work in the classroom.

## Why I Took It 🚀

I did this course ahead of my Visiting Demonstrator role and as part of my wider professional development. As a Business Information Technology student with an interest in education, I wanted to understand how teachers help students stay adaptable, not just what content to cover.

The course made me think about *how* people learn to solve problems and work on real projects, which lines up with how I've approached my own studies.

## Key Skills Developed 💡

- **Business literacy**: Entrepreneurship and market basics for learners
- **Video production literacy**: Storytelling and visual communication
- **Coding literacy**: Computational thinking and digital problem-solving
- **Pedagogical strategies**: Project-based learning and creativity in the classroom
- **Future skills framework**: Skills that transfer across changing job markets
- **Educational technology**: Using tech meaningfully in teaching

## How I've Used It 🔧

- Approaching complex problems with structure and creativity
- Communicating ideas through different media
- Seeing business and technology from a teaching angle
- Applying project-based ideas to my own coursework
- Thinking about which skills to build for the long term

## What's Next? 🔮

My main focus is still Business Information Technology, but the ideas feed into work where training, change management, and clear communication matter: tech integration, project management, and digital transformation projects.
