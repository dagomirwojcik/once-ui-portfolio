## Overview

"Folding" in our community refers to running simulations of proteins, the molecular machines that perform most of the active processes we associate with life, from muscle contraction to sensing light and digesting food. We exploit the biological insight these simulations provide to inform drug discovery and other efforts to combat global health threats.

As a volunteer Citizen Scientist, I contributed my personal computing resources to Folding@Home's distributed computing network. This project harnesses the collective power of thousands of volunteers worldwide to simulate protein folding dynamics, helping researchers understand disease mechanisms and develop potential treatments for conditions like Alzheimer's, cancer, and COVID-19.

My participation involved running specialized software that performed complex protein folding calculations during idle computer time, contributing valuable computational cycles to advance scientific research without disrupting my daily workflow.
