## What is Project FrameON?

FrameON is an innovative smart picture frame project developed for Birmingham City University's Innovation Fest 2024. As the digital innovation industry continues to evolve, businesses must stay abreast of the latest trends and leverage innovative solutions to effectively reach and engage their target audiences. FrameON operates within this dynamic landscape, offering a smart picture frame designed to promote environmental sustainability through the principles of reduce, reuse, and recycle.

### Project Overview

FrameON represents a convergence of technology and environmental consciousness, creating an interactive digital frame that serves as both a functional display device and an educational tool for sustainable living. The project addresses the growing need for eco-friendly technology solutions while maintaining practical utility in modern homes and businesses.

#### Key Features & Innovation

The smart picture frame incorporates current technology constraints while maximizing impact through:

- **Sustainable Design Philosophy**: Built around the core principles of environmental responsibility
- **Smart Display Technology**: Leverages existing digital frame technology for enhanced user interaction
- **Educational Integration**: Serves as a platform for promoting sustainable living practices
- **Modular Architecture**: Designed for easy maintenance and component reuse

#### Team Leadership & Development

As Project Manager, I led a diverse team of five talented individuals through the complete development lifecycle, from initial concept ideation to final presentation at Innovation Fest 2024. Our collaborative approach ensured that each team member's expertise contributed to the project's success, resulting in a cohesive solution that effectively communicates our environmental message through innovative technology.

The project demonstrates the power of interdisciplinary collaboration in creating solutions that address real-world challenges while pushing the boundaries of what's possible within current technological constraints.
