## Project Context & Background

During my Level 3 BTEC Diploma in IT studies, I gained experience across multiple IT sectors including programming, game design, and animation. BTECs focus heavily on practical work, making real-world project experience invaluable for personal statements and CVs. When our college partnered with Career Colleges Trust for a digital project sponsored by industry giants, it presented an exceptional opportunity for hands-on learning and professional development.

### About Career Colleges Trust

The Career Colleges Trust is a charity dedicated to supporting students in building successful careers. They achieve this by connecting employers and education providers to give students the skills, knowledge, and experience needed to succeed in the real world of work. While focusing primarily on IT, they also specialize in other professions such as Business.

Career Colleges employs a project-based learning approach where the same brief is given to student groups across multiple colleges, creating a nationwide competition. This innovative educational model aligns with the transition from Foundation/Extended Diplomas to T-Levels (2-year courses equivalent to 3 A-Levels) in specialized sector areas.

### Project Environment & Challenges

The project unfolded during the challenging period of the second and third national lockdowns, requiring all workshops and meetings to be conducted online. Despite these constraints, the virtual format provided valuable experience in remote collaboration and digital project management.

### Project Journey & Experience

### Phase 1: Project Initiation & Briefing

Working as a Career Colleges student in 2021, we underwent a comprehensive series of workshops designed to ensure we delivered an exceptional solution. Our detailed timetable coordinated workshops, meetings, and key events throughout the project lifecycle.

During Phase 1, we met Career Colleges representative Julia Von Klonowski, who explained the significance of T-Levels and their future benefits. She then briefed us on our project challenge: developing a solution that would compete head-to-head against other groups and colleges nationwide.

### The Challenge & Competition

We faced the daunting task of impressing a prestigious panel of judges from industry giants including:
- **Career Colleges, Amazon Web Services, BBC, HSBC**
- **Barnardo's, Prince's Trust, Lord's (Cricket)**
- **London Sport, UK Active, The Metropolitan Police**

With 10 judges from diverse companies, creating a solution that resonated with everyone was no easy feat. We dedicated significant time to brainstorming names, concepts, and technical approaches, ultimately deciding on developing a mobile application.

### Professional Development Workshops

Our comprehensive training program included:

- **Goal Mapping Workshop**: Strategic planning from point A to point B
- **Personal Branding Workshop**: Professional image and employer appeal
- **Product Design Workshop**: Analyzing successful products and user preferences
- **Digital Marketing Workshop**: Product promotion and market positioning

These workshops provided both personal and professional development, directly enhancing our project solution capabilities.

### Phase 2: Development & Team Structure

Two groups from Palmer's College and one from Seevic College advanced to Phase 2, where we began intensive project development. This phase emphasized effective collaboration and leveraging individual team strengths.

#### Team Organization & Leadership

As Project Manager/Organizer, I established a structured team approach:
- **Head of Design**: Visual and user experience development
- **Head Programmer**: Technical implementation and development
- **R&D Team**: Additional workforce for research and development
- **Creative Tagline**: "Team of six, workforce of seven"

Our Phase 2 presentation covered six key areas: About the Project, Source of Motivation, Design Insight, Sneak Peak, Collaborative Technologies, and Sponsor Input.

### Final Presentation & Recognition

Due to COVID-19 restrictions, our final presentation was recorded in the Immersive Room and submitted to judges, replacing the planned Amazon Offices presentation in Shoreditch.

#### Project Success & Awards

On June 24th, Team Zeta achieved remarkable success:
- **Team Award**: "Best Run Project"
- **Individual Award**: "USP Career College Student of the Year" (Dagomir Wójcik)

This hard-fought victory against unknown competition from other colleges demonstrated our team's exceptional execution and my leadership capabilities in maximizing efficiency through strategic team management.

### Project Zeta: Solution Overview

### The Challenge Brief

Our project began with a specific brief: *"Use the positive power of technology to promote an understanding about the mental and physical effects of COVID-19 on your age group and how it impacts your concerns about your future. Promote solutions to these issues in relation to your age group and the community."*

This assignment positioned us to develop a solution that could provide significant advantages for our future careers while addressing real-world challenges faced by our generation.

### Project Zeta Concept

Project Zeta represents our innovative response to the pandemic's social isolation challenges. We envisioned an app that transcends traditional social media, focusing on rebuilding the communication devastation caused by COVID-19 lockdowns and restrictions.

#### Core Vision & Mission

> "Our app is designed to help students feel like part of a community although we can't go out to parties and events. This app will make it so that people can set up events for themselves and others, whether these events are for socializing or educational purposes. This will help people feel they aren't alone and that others are experiencing them as well." – Team Zeta

### Key Features & Functionality

#### Youth-Focused Design
- **Age-Restricted Platform**: Exclusively designed for our age group
- **Youth-Centric Interface**: Created entirely with young people in mind
- **Peer-to-Peer Connection**: Facilitating genuine social interaction

#### Event Management System
- **Event Creation**: Users can set up events for themselves and others
- **Diverse Event Types**: Socializing, educational, and support-focused events
- **Smart Tag System**: Searchable categories for games, hobbies, and support groups
- **Professional Integration**: Judges and future partners can post workshops, events, and employment opportunities

#### Mental Health & Wellbeing Focus
- **Community Building**: Addressing pandemic-induced isolation
- **Support Networks**: Connecting people who need someone to talk to
- **Motivation Restoration**: Helping those whose motivation dropped during the pandemic
- **Wellbeing Priority**: Emphasizing the importance of mental health

### Technical Implementation & Future Vision

While we recognized that college students couldn't develop a fully completed application, we created a comprehensive concept that demonstrated clear direction and purpose. The project addressed the critical need for community connection during a time when many young people struggled with mental health challenges and educational motivation.

Project Zeta ultimately represents more than just a mobile application – it's a solution that addresses the fundamental human need for connection, support, and community, especially during challenging times when traditional social interaction becomes limited.
